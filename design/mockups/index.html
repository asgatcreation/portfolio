Notes on these mockups

  • All facts come from your repos, checked on 11 November 2026. The response time is a placeholder, [[ADD: response time]], because you haven’t told me one.
  • The drawing is live Brownian motion: about 7 seconds to draw, then it redraws. It pauses off-screen and stays still for “reduce motion” users and Save-Data connections. The real site also gets a static SVG for when JavaScript is off.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts here. The real site self-hosts them, subset to the characters we use, for speed.
  • Only CarHub has screenshots in your repos so far, so some cards in mockup C show “Screenshot to come”.
  • Status colours (live, in development, pre-release) are colour-blind safe and also differ in shape: a full, half and diamond dot.
