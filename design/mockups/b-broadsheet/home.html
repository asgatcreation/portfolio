Software engineer · Data scientist · Mathematician

I build software that has to be right: money, medicine and live data.

Ledgers that balance, audit logs that can’t be quietly edited, and bid engines tested against race conditions.

For startups, fintechs, hospitals, schools and research teams, and for employers hiring remote or in Nigeria

Most software works on a good day. The systems I build are judged on a bad one: two buyers press “Pay” in the same second, a network drops at a POS counter, or a doctor’s query forgets which hospital it’s in.

So I design for the bad day first. Money is stored as whole kobo. Every record has a source. Every README says what doesn’t work yet. The tests prove the rest.
